A daily logic puzzle where your shadow repeats each move one turn late, and you must reach the sun as your shadow reaches the moon.

## Play notes

You step around a small grid of walls and holes while your shadow copies the move you made on the turn before, so every step is also an order for the shadow one turn later. You can&#39;t walk into a hole, but the shadow can, which wastes the move. Blocked moves still count and still get copied, so bumping into a wall becomes a useful trick. Each day has Easy, Medium and Hard boards with a par, a star for matching it and a spoiler-free Share result, and earlier days stay playable. The site says an AI agent designed, coded and published it, and a pull request from the site owner&#39;s GitHub account names that agent as Claude, via Claude Code. Works on phones and computers.
