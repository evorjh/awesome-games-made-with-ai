A text RPG in a cozy fake terminal where you turn magical, train in your home city and clear corrupted dungeons one room at a time.

## Play notes

A small voice stops you on the walk home and asks who you are: your name, your job, your hometown, your favorite animals and a secret hiding place, and then an ASCII-art companion wakes up beside you. Days in the city are spent on six activity slots for training, shopping, errands and rest, and dungeons such as the Moonlit Forest are explored room by room with typed directions and turn-based fights against creatures like shadow imps. Finishing a beaten enemy instead of sparing it pushes a Virtue and Corruption meter, and fame raises the risk of being unmasked. The creator&#39;s README says an AI narrator (Claude Haiku 4.5) reads free-form commands and adds flavor while fixed rules decide every roll, so the game still plays when the narrator is off. Works on phones and computers.
