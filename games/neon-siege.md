## Play notes

You steer a small glowing craft around a neon arena ringed by pylons and hold off waves of robots: wave 1 sends Skitter drones and wave 2 brings Gunner units, and the creator&#39;s notes promise an Overseer boss on every fifth wave. You aim with the mouse and hold to fire, dash through bots without taking damage, and raise a shield that bounces their bolts back but keeps you from shooting. Weapon pickups such as the Pulse Rifle, Rail Lance and Nova Launcher come with limited ammo, and you fall back on an unlimited Blaster when they run dry. Best on a computer: it needs a keyboard and mouse.
