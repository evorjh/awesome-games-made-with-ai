- **Genre:** Shooter, Action
- **Plays on:** Computer
- **Price:** Free
- **Controls:** WASD to move, mouse to aim and shoot, F for your class gadget, Q for the sentry turret, G for a grenade, 1 and 2 to switch weapons, Tab for the score, Esc for the menu
- **Likes on the original post:** Unknown

A conquest shooter where four parody AI-lab factions fight over capture points, 4v4 up to 12v12, against bots in your browser or online in a beta.
