## Play notes

You walk a dark pine forest dotted with log cabins, looking for seven blue flowers that only show up in your flashlight beam. The catch is that spirits can only see you while the light is on, and its battery drains as you use it, so you switch it on and off as you go. A spirit that reaches you ends the run, and that can happen within the first minute. At launch the creator said the first version took three hours and that the camera was a bit buggy, especially on mobile. Works on phones and computers: phones get a joystick and a look pad.
