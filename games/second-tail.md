A rainy-city platformer about two pet rats walking home, where you swap between one rat&#39;s double jump and the other&#39;s dash and ram.

## Play notes

Two pet rats, Sal and Ben, climb out of a carrier left outside a vet clinic and set off for home through a painted city at rat height. An old gray rat on a ledge teaches the double jump before the first tall wall, Q swaps which rat leads, and past the wall the path opens onto a riverside promenade with cheese to collect. The creator lists rooftops, a mine, a market, several bosses and an eight-lane race in this early demo, and says the art is AI-made and animated in code, with music from Suno and Freesound. The first load is about 190 MB. Best on a computer: it needs a keyboard.
