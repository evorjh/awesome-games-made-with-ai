## Prompt

```text
Create a visually stunning small game centered around an original mechanical creature.
The game takes place high in a vast snowy mountain environment with steep peaks, cliffs, valleys, deep snow, rocks and open areas to explore.
I want an original creature that feels genuinely engineered rather than a normal animal covered in metal. Its body should be made from interconnected mechanical parts, articulated joints, pistons, linkages, cables and moving components that appear to have a real purpose.
The creature should have a strong, intimidating silhouette and enough mechanical detail that close-up views are interesting.
I should be able to directly control the creature and freely explore the mountains.
The creature must be able to:
walk naturally
run with a convincing sense of speed and weight
jump
climb and move across uneven terrain
recover naturally when landing or losing balance

fly through the mountains
transition smoothly between ground movement and flight
Its feet should find the terrain naturally, its body should react to slopes and obstacles, and the mechanical structure should respond believably to movement.
Flying should feel especially satisfying. I want to be able to launch from a mountain or cliff, gain altitude, fly between peaks, dive into valleys and land back on the terrain.
Make the environment feel atmospheric and beautiful: huge snowy mountains, distant peaks, changing elevation, clouds, wind, snow and a strong sense of scale.
Do not rely on a pre-made creature or canned animation. The creature, its movement and its mechanical behavior should be created specifically for this project.
The final experience should make the creature feel heavy, physical, intricate, powerful and slightly terrifying, while still being genuinely fun to control.
Prioritize visual quality, responsive controls, believable movement, mechanical detail, atmosphere and the feeling of exploring a massive mountain world.
Use whatever approach and tools you believe will produce the strongest result.
Take creative control over the creature's design, environment, movement, camera, lighting, sound and overall atmosphere. Make ambitious choices.
Before considering it finished, actually play the game yourself. Run around, jump, climb, fly between the mountains and land again. Inspect it critically and improve anything that feels fake, broken, weightless, awkward or visually weak.
Use can use 2-3 sub agents at max if you want but dont let them spawn more agents please
```
