## Play notes

You play a capybara courier on a scooter, accepting orders in the CapyFood app on your in-game phone, picking up items at the market and carrying them across a city of towers, hill roads and beaches watched over by a capybara Christ the Redeemer. Items in your delivery box can fall out if you ride roughly, and you can drift, pull wheelies and honk on the way. Play Offline keeps the city to yourself, Play Online shares it with other players, and the current version wears a Halloween theme. The game credits Claude Code for the coding, Suno for the music, ElevenLabs for sound effects and Tripo3D for 3D rigging, and it won Vibe Jam 2026. Best on a computer: it needs a keyboard and mouse.
