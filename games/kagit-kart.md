A kart racer inside a pop-up book, with paper animal drivers, eight karts on the grid and stationery items like pen rockets and scissors.

## Play notes

You race paper karts across the pages of a pop-up book, and the scenery folds up out of the page as you drive toward it. There are Grand Prix, single race and time trial modes, three speed classes named after paper weights, and eight animal drivers, from Cinnamon the fox to Inky the octopus. The four chapters run from Daisy Meadow to Bosphorus Night, Glacier Pass and a ruled-notebook road across The Writing Desk. Holding Space while steering drifts for a boost. The creator says the meshes, textures, sounds and music are generated by code with no asset files, and the menus come in English or Turkish. Works on phones and computers.
