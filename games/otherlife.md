A Chinese-language pixel life sim that draws a random birth anywhere in the world, then lets you live that life year by year.

## Play notes

You draw a birth by lot from 172 countries and regions, weighted by real birth numbers, and the game reveals your name, birthplace and family one card at a time. Then the years roll on in real time, about a minute per year, while a pixel scene of your family and home changes as you grow, relatives chat in a side panel and your health, happiness, knowledge and relationship bars shift. Major choices pause the clock, or you can switch on automatic choices and speed up to 16x. The interface is in Chinese only. Works on phones and computers.
