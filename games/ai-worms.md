## Play notes

Its creator directed Claude through more than 140 versions of this game, and says Claude made all of it: the code, the drawings, the sounds and the music, all drawn and synthesized in the browser with no image or audio files. When an appliance in the kitchen grows glowing eyes you tap it before the worm tunnels away; five escapes and the house is theirs, though you can continue from the level you reached. Linked worms pop together, worms caught mid-air score double, and tapping the friendly characters gives a bonus instead of a penalty. The worlds run from the living room and garage to Dracula&#39;s castle, outer space and Neon Tokyo. The creator made it for AI Browser Game Jam 5 and says it plays best on a phone held upright.
