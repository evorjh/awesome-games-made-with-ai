A turn-based 4X that starts on Earth, where reaching space turns your planet into one tile of the Solar System and later of the Galaxy.

## Play notes

You found cities on a hex map of Earth, research technologies, build units and deal with computer rivals, then build a Space Expedition to move up a level. Your whole planet becomes a single tile on the Solar System map and keeps producing for you there, and the same happens again when you reach the Galaxy. You can also start straight in the Solar System or Galaxy age, and a short guide walks you through the first turns. The creator says Grok helped with the final review and Suno made the music. Works on phones and computers.
