A time-loop puzzle across six vaults, where your earlier runs come back as ghosts that hold down pressure plates.

## Play notes

Each vault is a small grid with a diamond to reach, doors marked with letters and plates that open them while someone stands on them. Each step is one tick, and pressing Loop sends you back to the start while your run so far replays as a ghost, so you can park one copy of yourself on a plate and walk through the door with the next. Later vaults have doors that need several plates at once, and a solver sets the par for the fewest ghosts. There are only six vaults, so it is a short sitting. Works on phones and computers: phones get an on-screen pad.
