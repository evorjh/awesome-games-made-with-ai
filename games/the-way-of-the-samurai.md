A rain-soaked samurai action game where a lone ronin fights waves of bandits with combos, parries, rolls and finishing moves.

## Play notes

You play a ronin in Japan in 1600, just after a great war, cutting through waves of bandits on a rain-soaked, flooded road in Chapter I, The Flooded Field. Fights mix light and heavy cuts into combos, with rolls, timed parries, a guard that strains under pressure, kunai throws and finishing moves, and the game offers Stone, Water and Wind stances. Scroll cards in Japanese and English tell the story between fights. The creator says Claude made the first version in under an hour from an AI video they had made, and that they then spent 12 more hours on the gameplay; the in-game credits also list Codex. Best on a computer: it needs a keyboard and mouse or a gamepad.
