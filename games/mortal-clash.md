A one-on-one 3D fighting game against a CPU opponent, with five fighters, four difficulty levels, combos, specials and finishing moves.

## Play notes

You pick one of five fighters, such as Kage or Vega, and a CPU difficulty from Novice to Grandmaster, then fight in rounds on a temple bridge under a red moon. Punches, kicks and blocks chain into combos, crouching turns them into uppercuts and sweeps, and each fighter has a special move and a fatality. It borrows openly from Mortal Kombat, from its finishing moves to its title screen, and the fighters are stock 3D models from the Three.js examples, which the creator credits. Best on a computer: it needs a keyboard.
