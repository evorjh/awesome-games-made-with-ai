A neon space shooter where you fly a fighter around a Dyson sphere and shoot down the alien ships attacking it.

## Play notes

You fly a fighter in first person around a star wrapped in a glowing wireframe Dyson sphere, moving in three dimensions and firing lasers at the alien ships closing in on it. A cockpit display tracks your hull, your boost and the sphere&#39;s shield, with a radar of incoming threats and a comms log, and the run ends once the shield and your hull give out. The creator says he started it in Claude Code, moved to Cursor and then to gptme, and later had Grok plan a rewrite of the code. Works on phones and computers, with an on-screen stick and fire and boost buttons on phones.
