A trade-show tycoon game where you build tables, sign exhibitors and run show days, growing from one meeting room to full exhibition halls.

## Play notes

You start in a small meeting room with one empty spot: walk onto the tile and a table builds itself in a burst of confetti, and the next task sends you to meet your first exhibitor. From there, the game&#39;s guide says, you badge visitors in, fetch coffee and walk VIPs to sponsors on show day, then spend the earnings on bigger rooms, exhibition halls and shows in Singapore and Macao. It adds that staff you hire take over each job in turn, and that seasons running from full moon to full moon end in a Grand Expo with a leaderboard. It is made by Messe Asia, a Bangkok company that runs real trade shows, and the in-game shop sells only cosmetics. Works on phones and computers.
