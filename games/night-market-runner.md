Dash between the food stalls of a Taiwanese night market, picking up orders and handing them to hungry customers to earn enough before the clock runs out.

## Play notes

You run deliveries in a busy Taiwanese night market lit with red lanterns, picking up dishes at the stalls and carrying them to the customers who ordered them. The first of its three levels gives you 60 seconds to earn $350, and you switch between the dishes you&#39;re holding with Q and E. The creator made it with Claude Code in a tutorial video, with the art, characters and scenes generated by Higgsfield. The game is in Traditional Chinese and needs a keyboard.
