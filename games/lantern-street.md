- **Made with:** Claude, OpenRouter
- **Where they name the AI:** [“Claude asked me lots of design questions, produced a written game bible (~12k words of script so far), a design system, all the code.”](https://www.reddit.com/r/ClaudeAI/comments/1xn6w4z/asked_claude_for_a_little_boba_shop_game_for_my/)
- **Creator:** [@earofgeorge](https://www.reddit.com/user/earofgeorge/)
- **Genre:** Cozy, Simulation
- **Plays on:** Computer
- **Price:** Free
- **Controls:** Click ingredients, ice and sweetness, then the shaker and a customer to serve; click dialogue choices and shop menus
- **Likes on the original post:** 64, counted 2026-11-13

Mix boba orders through the evening rush, then learn your regulars’ stories and recover the missing pages of your great-aunt’s recipe book.
