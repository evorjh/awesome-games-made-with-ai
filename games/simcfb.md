A college football coaching game where you recruit, bid for NIL, work the transfer portal, set game plans and watch or call every snap in 3D.

## Play notes

You coach one of 68 major college programs for as many seasons as you like, handling the recruiting board, the NIL budget, the transfer portal, weekly practice plans and your staff. On game day you choose how much to do yourself, from simming straight to the final score to picking formation and play for every snap, and each play runs out with 3D players in a full stadium. A Quick game mode skips the career and drops you into a single matchup, and nothing needs an account. Teams appear under their real school names and conferences with made-up mascots. Works on phones turned sideways and on computers.
