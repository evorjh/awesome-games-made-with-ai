A cozy cat-care game where you merge wood, fabric and food into treats, beds and toys for a cat named Cream.

## Play notes

You look after Cream, a cream-and-ginger cat, in a garden and a small cottage. On a twelve-slot merge board you pair matching wood, fabric and food items, which refill on timers, until they reach a level you can craft into treats, beds or toys, then feed Cream or place furniture to raise your Bond. A daily-life panel opens a garden match-3, commissions, cat-play challenges and a story journal of memories, and clicking open ground walks Cream around. The creator says Codex wrote most of the game-specific scripting and OpenAI image generation helped with the art, while the music came from the project owner. Best on a computer: it needs a mouse and plays in a tall frame.
