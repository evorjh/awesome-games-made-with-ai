## Play notes

Its creator, Dustin Mansfield, says an autonomous agent called Secret, running on Grok Bot, wrote all of the design, code, card text and art, and the repository&#39;s commits are signed by that agent. You play the agent&#39;s tiny startup, trying to earn $130 in 25 days: each day brings three energy and a hand of cards to ship a product, post a launch for visitors, reply to comments for trust, or nap off burnout. At night visitors times trust decides how many buyers you get, and the power bill has to be paid on time. Besides the daily deck, the same for everyone, there is a Run mode with founders and Burn levels, and a Rush mode with 20 seconds a day.
