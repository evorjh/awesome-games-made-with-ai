A third-person wuxia sword-fighting game in three chapters: a golden steppe, a bamboo forest in a night storm and a lantern-lit canal town.

## Play notes

You play a lone swordsman who fights his way through three chapters, from a steppe of tall golden grass at dusk to a bamboo forest in a rainstorm and a canal town lit by lanterns, each with a boss at the end. Groups of straw-hatted bandits close in, and you answer with light and heavy combos, dodges, a lock-on, a ranged sword-qi strike and blocks that turn into parries if timed on impact. The creator says Claude Opus 5.5 wrote the code, while the character models came from Tripo and most of the animation is Adobe Mixamo motion capture. The screens are in Chinese with English captions. Works on phones and computers; the touch controls are meant for a phone held sideways.
