A party racer for two to eight on one shared screen, with drifting, items and bots, where phones or keyboards serve as controllers.

## Play notes

One computer shows the race and creates a room, and each player joins with a keyboard or by opening the controller page on a phone; empty places on the grid can go to bots. The car accelerates by itself, so you steer, drift through the bends and fire the items you pick up, while a shared camera knocks out anyone who falls too far behind. Races run on Sundown Junction, Timber Run or Aloha Inferno, and each round moves points between the racers until someone reaches the target. Best on a computer: the race needs one shared screen, with keyboards or phones as controllers.
