- **Made with:** Claude Opus 5.5
- **Where they name the AI:** [“I directed and designed most of the game, and Claude (Opus 5.5) on medium wrote the code: about 24,000 lines of JavaScript with three.js, packaged with Electron.”](https://www.reddit.com/r/aigamedev/comments/1xr2952/kasumi_courier_a_90sstyle_cyberpunk_delivery_game/)
- **Creator:** [@burgric](https://www.reddit.com/user/burgric)
- **Genre:** Simulation, Open world, Racing
- **Plays on:** Computer
- **Price:** Free demo; the full game is paid
- **Controls:** W and S to drive and brake, A and D to steer, Space for the handbrake, F to get out or use things, WASD and the mouse on foot, Tab for the map, J for the job board; the full list is under Controls
- **Likes on the original post:** 43, counted 2026-11-15

Deliver parcels by moped through a rain-soaked 90s cyberpunk city at night, earning a reputation one review at a time.
