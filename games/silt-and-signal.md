## Play notes

A healer warns that Neri&#39;s light is fading, and your first task is to ride a hover-bike through a sunlit cliff village to her gatehouse. Its page says the journey then carries Neri through seven worlds, from broken aqueducts to midnight forests and sky orchards, with voiced scenes, and that free-ride and racing modes are there too. A marker and a minimap show the way. The creator gave Claude Opus 5.5 and GPT-6.1 Sol the same brief to compare them and built the game with Crayon and Three.js, but the post doesn&#39;t say which model made the version it links.
