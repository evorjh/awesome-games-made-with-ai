## Prompt

```text
/game-studio:new I want to build a murder mystery game that runs in the browser. You're on a 1936 sleeper train stopped overnight by a snow slide, someone's been murdered, and you have to interview six suspects before the sheriff arrives at dawn. Every suspect is voiced, so you catch liars by how they sound. Make it look cinematic, with real 3D rooms.
```
