## Play notes

You arrive at a coastal observatory where the clock stopped at 00:17 and explore its dome, archive, signal room and tidal well by clicking glowing objects. A phase instrument flips each room between the Present and its Echo, the minute before, and the two often hold different records. Logs and inscriptions go into a field journal, and you use them to work instruments such as a receiver whose three radio bands must be tuned to one moment. There are four chapters and 13 puzzles, in English or Chinese. The creator says GPT-6 Astra worked on the story, puzzles, text and code over several rounds, and that the seven painted backgrounds were made separately with OpenAI&#39;s image generator. Works on phones and computers.
