## Play notes

You play a tiny courier who wakes up on a giant&#39;s desk at midnight, where ink bottles, teacups, a globe and a typewriter are the terrain. Pencils become bridges, levers raise passages and stacks of books become platforms, and when the giant&#39;s hand reaches down you crouch behind cover to stay unseen. The creator had Kimi K3.1 write all the code and Tripo make the 3D models, and says the game borrows how Little Nightmares plays while its scenes are original. It needs WebGL 2 and a keyboard, and it saves your progress.
