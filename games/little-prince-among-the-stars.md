A 3D story game that follows the little prince from planet to planet, starting on his tiny home asteroid with its volcanoes and rose.

## Play notes

You walk the little prince around B-612, his home asteroid, with smoking volcanoes, tall grass full of flowers and a sea of clouds below the edge. Chapters are laid out as planets on a watercolor journey map, and only the first is open when you start. Each chapter opens with a short storybook card before you step onto the planet. The creator says the AI turned each planet in the story into an explorable 3D scene over 16 days, working with Tripo and Blender. Best on a computer: it needs a keyboard and mouse, and the game says phones and tablets aren&#39;t supported yet.
