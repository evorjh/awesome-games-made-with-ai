## Play notes

You drive a rocket car around an enclosed stadium and try to knock an oversized ball into the other goal, using boost pads, jumps, flips, air rolls and wall driving. Matches are 1v1 against a bot or 2v2 with a bot teammate, at Rookie, Pro or All-Star skill, and there is a practice mode with an empty field. Ramming the other car at supersonic speed demolishes it, and a ball cam keeps the ball in view. The creator says the physics follow figures measured by the Rocket League community, and an online mode lets two players meet with a room code. Best on a computer: it needs a keyboard or a gamepad.
