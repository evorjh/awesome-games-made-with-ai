- **Genre:** Sports, Arcade
- **Plays on:** Computer and phone
- **Price:** Free
- **Controls:** W A S D or arrows to move, Space to sprint, J to pass or switch player, hold K to charge a shot, L to juke or slide tackle, I to lob; gamepads and a touch joystick work too
- **Likes on the original post:** Unknown

A fast five-a-side football game against the computer: pick two national teams, then pass, charge shots that curl and dip, and slide in for tackles.
