A cozy astrophotography sim where you set up a camera and tripod at a lakeside camp, wait for dark and photograph the Milky Way and Orion.

## Play notes

You arrive at a camp by Lake Aurelia, in New Zealand&#39;s Mackenzie Basin, at 17:20 with a van, a campfire and a case of camera gear, and a nine-step checklist walks you through the setup: tripod, camera, lens, battery and card, manual mode and focus. Pressing T skips ahead to moments such as blue hour, astronomical night or the time Orion clears the eastern hills, and the sky fills with stars that the game says come from real catalogs. Your field notes set two main shots, the Milky Way core and Orion, plus bonus challenges like star trails, a meteor or a satellite crossing the frame. The creator says Claude Sonnet 5.5 made it in one shot over about five hours. Best on a computer: it needs a keyboard and mouse.
