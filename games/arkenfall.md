An open-world action game in a meadow valley where terrain, creatures and music are all generated by code.

## Play notes

You wake beside a campfire in Hearthmoor, a meadow by a river, and head out across an open valley toward a distant spire. Apart from its cover image, everything in the world, from terrain and creatures to the music, is generated by code the AI wrote. Fights reward well-timed dodges and parries, and waystones let you travel back along the paths you&#39;ve opened. Best on a computer: it needs a keyboard and mouse.
