## Play notes

You choose a tree from a nursery of maples, pines, cherries and more, then tend it on a bench with five tools: water, prune, trim, wire and decorate. Time runs or pauses at your command, and the tree&#39;s color, fullness and a health readout show how stressed it is, so overwatering or cutting too much at once can make it wither. Pruning mode strips the foliage back to show the branches and buds, and you can switch the scene between a zen garden, misty dawn, cherry dusk and moonlit night. The creator says Claude Opus 4.7 designed the 3D assets and Suno made the music. Best on a computer: it needs a mouse.
