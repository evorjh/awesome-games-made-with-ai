## Play notes

You pick one of eight racers, such as Blaze the fox, and an engine class from 50cc to 150cc, then race seven AI rivals in a Grand Prix cup, a single VS race or a time trial. The Turbo Cup runs three laps on each of four tracks (Sunshine Circuit, Dune Canyon, Frost Peak and Neon Skyway). The first is a meadow course past grandstands and windmills. Space or Shift hops into a drift, and a loading tip explains that holding the item button trails a Peel or Bouncer behind you as a shield. The creator says Claude Opus 5.5 built it in one go, in about five hours. On phones it switches to on-screen steering and an auto-accelerate button. Works on phones and computers.
