A 3D sailing sim where you steer and trim the sail to the wind, with a new race course each day, open-ocean runs and a shared sea.

## Play notes

You helm a small sailboat among low-poly islands, steering with the rudder and easing or tightening the sail as the wind shifts. The daily race sets a new shared course each day, with a countdown to the start line and ghost boats from earlier runs to race against, and High Sea sends you through big open-ocean swells to collect buoys while the wind and waves build. Free sailing puts you in an open sea with other players, and a tutorial covers steering, sail trim and racing. The creator says he first built it with Claude 3.5 Sonnet and has since worked on it mostly with Codex models and lately with Grok in Cursor. Works on phones and computers.
