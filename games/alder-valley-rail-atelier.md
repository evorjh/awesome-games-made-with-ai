## Play notes

A steam engine called Nightingale pulls a passenger train around a model railway set on a table in a room lined with bookshelves. You set its regulator, sound the whistle and call a station stop, and watch from the room, trackside, a follow camera or the cab, or jump to views of the viaduct, old town, harbor, summit and engine yard. Build mode adds cottages, a church, a windmill, a station, a signal cabin and other pieces, lets you reshape the track itself, and saves your layout in the browser. There are no goals or scores: it is a toy railway to tinker with, and the page also points to the creator&#39;s larger follow-up, Whistlevale. Works on phones and computers.
