A short ASCII horror game where you pilot a submersible into an uncharted cave beneath the Antarctic ice in 1972.

## Play notes

You play Dr. Harlan Webb, steering the HALDANE-4 submersible down into a cavity under the Ross Ice Shelf to reach 1,325 meters and bring back a sample. The cave walls, your craft and the cable trailing back up to the surface are drawn in text characters on an old terminal screen. You thrust through the dark, turn the view to find passages that only show from some angles, and can cut your lamp and engine to hide. The creator says a run takes 10 to 15 minutes, the caves and sound are generated in code and the radio voice was made with ElevenLabs; it won Most Original at Vibe Jam 2026. Works on phones and computers, though the creator recommends a computer and headphones.
