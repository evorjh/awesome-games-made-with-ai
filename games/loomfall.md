An idle RPG where your hero fights on its own along a route you set across the map, while you craft gear from reagents and spend skill points.

## Play notes

Its creator spent three years on an idle RPG from 2016, then had Claude remake it from scratch with new ideas. You pick one of five classes, from a Weaver who casts every element to a Necromancer with skeleton archers, and the hero fights by itself while you choose where it goes: clearing a place reveals the paths beyond it, and a route lets it walk on alone. The creator says gear drops are rare, so most progress comes from crafting with the reagents you collect, alongside a passive skill tree, a village and a rift. It is laid out like a phone app, and the game says combat goes on even while it is closed.
