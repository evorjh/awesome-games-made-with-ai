A pixel-art tank war where you build factories and towers around your pyramid, click enemy tanks to hurt them and push to destroy the red pyramid.

## Play notes

Your blue pyramid sits on one side of a desert map and the red one on the other: you place a factory that rolls out tanks, towers for defense and income buildings that mine crystals, while clicking enemy tanks yourself to chip away at them. Enemy waves arrive within the first minute, ability cards like Storm can turn a fight, and booster packs from the card shop add new units and abilities between battles. The free browser demo covers campaign levels 1 to 4, a Mirror Duel and the card shop, with levels 5 to 10 kept for the paid full version. The creator says the sounds and graphics were also made with Claude, in plain HTML and JavaScript. Best on a computer: it needs a mouse and keyboard.
