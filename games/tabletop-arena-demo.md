- **Genre:** Strategy
- **Plays on:** Computer
- **Price:** Free demo; the full game is paid
- **Controls:** Click a figure, choose its action and click a square or target. Space ends the turn. Drag with the right mouse button to rotate the table.
- **Likes on the original post:** Unknown

Roll dice and move miniatures across a 3D tabletop. Each hit turns the dial on a figure’s base and changes what it can do.
