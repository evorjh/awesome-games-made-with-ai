An idle action RPG in low-poly dungeons where your hero&#39;s Spark fights on its own while you steer, dash and grab the loot.

## Play notes

You steer a small hero through the rooms of a dungeon such as the Hollow Crypt while your Spark attacks nearby monsters by itself, and you add a dash and a Nova blast for crowds. Monsters drop scrap and new dungeon maps, a sealed vault opens once you slay its rare monsters, and a pink arrow leads to the boss, with gold, silver and bronze times for clearing the map. A hero screen tracks your stats, bag, skills and a constellation, and the creator says hired helpers keep farming while you&#39;re away and that the player market never sells power for money. Works on phones and computers.
