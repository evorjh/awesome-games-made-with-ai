A free platform fighter where up to 20 players and bots try to knock each other off the arena, with eight fighters to pick from.

## Play notes

You choose one of eight fighters, such as the heavyweight Ballast or the acrobat Zephyr, and try to knock rivals off the stage; the more damage a fighter has taken, the farther a hit sends them. The &quot;Play with a friend&quot; option opens a private lobby that fills its empty seats with bots, so you can start a one-minute Timed Brawl even on your own, and public matches hold up to 20 fighters on an arena that shrinks as they drop out. A move list, a replay viewer and a local mode for two players on one keyboard are built in, and the code is open source. The project says an AI agent writes the code and runs the game day to day under a human owner. Works on phones and computers.
