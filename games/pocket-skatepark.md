## Play notes

You skate a sunny, cel-shaded park on the edge of a cartoon city, full of ramps, rails, ledges and a gap across the north plaza. Ollies, kickflips, heelflips, grabs, grinds and manuals link into lines that only bank when you land clean, and characters around the park offer bonus challenges you can accept or turn down. Score Attack gives you two minutes to post a score, Color Mode has you paint the ground by riding over it, and Free Skate has no clock. The creator credits Kenney for the game&#39;s assets. Best on a computer: it needs a keyboard or a controller.
