Drive a Cybertruck or an F1 car across an open desert, then fold it into a fighting robot at the press of a key.

## Play notes

You start beside a Cybertruck in an open desert and drive toward a walled city called Halcyon, drifting through sand that keeps your tire tracks. Press R and the truck unfolds into a robot that walks, jumps and fights, and Tab switches between six vehicles. It uses real car brands such as the Tesla Cybertruck and a Ferrari F1 car. The first load can take a while. The creator says it is still a work in progress and has published the source. Best on a computer: it needs a keyboard and mouse.
