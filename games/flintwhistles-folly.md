A point-and-click pirate comedy with a nine-verb interface, where would-be captain Rupert Flintwhistle sets out to relight a haunted lighthouse.

## Play notes

You play Rupert Flintwhistle, a hopeless would-be pirate captain, in a point-and-click comedy in the style of early-1990s adventure games: pick one of nine verbs such as Look at, Pick up or Talk to, then click something in the scene. It opens on a moonlit dock in Port Mildew beside a ship called the Leaky Tuna, where even looking at a seagull earns a joke, and the creator&#39;s page lists six parts of 30 to 60 minutes each. The creator says it uses no image or audio files: the pixel art, characters, music and sound are generated by code. It first came out as Scallywag Isle, and it plays free on Wavedash without an account. Best on a computer: it needs a mouse.
