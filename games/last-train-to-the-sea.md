A storybook tram game where you drive a small tram between hillside towns floating above the clouds and pick up travelers.

## Play notes

You drive Hearthwing, a small tram, along a railway that links storybook towns floating above the clouds, starting at Mango Tide with Folded Gardens as the next stop. At each station you open the doors for the travelers waiting on the platform, a counter shows how many are aboard, and an Explore town option sits beside the door button. You begin by picking one of four preset drivers for a driver ID card, with no account needed. It runs inside Crayon&#39;s arcade page and takes close to a minute to load. Best on a computer; Crayon&#39;s page says it also has one-hand phone controls, which we haven&#39;t tried.
