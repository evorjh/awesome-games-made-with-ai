Fourteen quick reflex, timing and memory minigames, each over in seconds, with a link that dares a friend to beat your round.

## Play notes

You pick one of 14 minigames, from Reaction Speed and Button Mash to Mole Smash, a 3D Cube Stack and a Home Run Derby, and each round lasts between 5 and 30 seconds after a short countdown. When it ends you get a challenge link, and the creator says a friend who opens it plays the exact same round, with the better score winning. Playing and sending challenges work without an account, and a short ad card sometimes shows before your result. Each game is tiny on its own, so it plays best as a quick back-and-forth with someone. Works on phones and computers.
