## Play notes

You start fourth of four on Coast Run, a 1.52 km road through pine woods on the Bay Coast at golden hour, and race three laps in a red Ferrari 458 Italia. The HUD tracks your position, lap time, gear and speed, and running wide onto the shoulder costs grip and flashes a warning. Space brakes, Shift pulls the handbrake, C switches the camera and R puts the car back on the road. The car carries the real model&#39;s name and badge. Best on a computer: it needs a keyboard.
