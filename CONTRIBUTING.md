# Contributing

Every game is checked against the rules below before it's added.

## What gets in

A game is listed when all of these are true:

- **AI wrote most of the code,** by the creator's own account, and the creator names the AI or the AI tool they used. A tool that belongs to one AI maker (Claude Code, Codex, Grok Build, Gemini CLI) credits that maker's AI. A tool that can run several makers' AIs, like Cursor, is credited as it is, and if the creator doesn't say which model, the entry says "model not named". Games where AI only made the art or music don't qualify.
- **It plays in a web browser now,** free or paid. Its entry says what it costs: free, free with in-game purchases, a free demo of a paid game, or paid. Video-only demos wait until something playable is out, and download-only games aren't listed yet.
- **It runs on its own.** Mods and ports that need another company's game files are left out.
- **It works:** it runs in desktop Chrome without game-breaking bugs.
- **It's good:** its original post has at least 1,000 likes, or it's worth at least a couple of minutes of play.
- **The creator is credited,** with a link to their original post.

Games that borrow a famous game's style are fine. Games that use another company's characters, names or brands are listed with a still image and a link, but are never featured.

## Submitting a game

[Open a submission](../../issues/new?template=submit-game.yml) with the play link and the post where the creator says which AI made it. Your own games are welcome.

## Asking for a change or removal

If you made a game that's listed and want something changed or the game removed, [open an issue](../../issues/new?template=change-or-removal.yml) or email the address in the README. The game is hidden the same day, and the entry is corrected or removed within a week. Copies of this repository that other people already made (forks) are outside our control.
